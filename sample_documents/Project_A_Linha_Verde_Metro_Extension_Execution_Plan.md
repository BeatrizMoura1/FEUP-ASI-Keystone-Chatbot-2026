## 1. Scope
Contoso is the lead designer for the 3.2 km extension of the Linha Verde metro line, from Alameda Norte to Quinta das Flores. The scope includes:
- 2.6 km of bored tunnel and 0.6 km of cut-and-cover tunnel,
- three new stations: Alameda Norte, Parque Tecnologico and Quinta das Flores,
- one ventilation shaft (VS-2) between Parque Tecnologico and Quinta das Flores,
- monitoring of existing buildings along the route during construction.
The construction contractor is Ferrovia Atlantica Construcoes (FAC).

## 5. Ground movement monitoring and trigger levels
Settlement of existing buildings is measured weekly. Trigger levels:
- Green: settlement below 10 mm. Normal monitoring.
- Amber: settlement between 10 mm and 20 mm. Monitoring becomes daily, and the Geotechnical Lead must review the data within 24 hours.
- Red: settlement above 20 mm, or a new crack wider than 2 mm in any building. Tunnelling in that zone stops. The Geotechnical Lead notifies the Project Director and the client's engineer, Eng. Paulo Mendes (VVM), within 2 hours.
