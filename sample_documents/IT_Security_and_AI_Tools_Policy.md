# Contoso Engineering Consultants: IT Security and AI Tools Policy

Project / visibility tag: GENERAL (all employees)
Document owner: IT Manager, Andre Lopes (itdesk@contoso.example)
Reference: IT-POL-002 | Revision: C02 | Last updated: 3 August 2026

## 1. Accounts and passwords
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the CDE and all project systems.
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Never reuse your Contoso password on another website.
- Never share your account with anyone, including colleagues covering for you during leave. Use delegation in Outlook instead.

## 2. Devices
- Only company laptops may be used to access client project data.
- Personal USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. Use the project CDE or the company file-sharing service to exchange files with clients.
- Laptops must be locked (Windows + L) whenever you leave your desk, including in the office.

## 3. Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT Service Desk within 1 hour, by calling extension 4357 or +351 210 000 435 outside office hours. IT will wipe the device remotely. A police report is required for stolen devices.

## 4. Phishing
- Use the "Report phishing" button in Outlook for any suspicious email. Do not forward it to colleagues.
- IT will never ask for your password by email or phone.
- If you clicked a suspicious link or entered your password, call the IT Service Desk immediately and change your password.

## 5. Use of AI tools
- Approved AI tools: the internal Keystone knowledge assistant and the company Microsoft Copilot licence.
- Never paste client data, drawings, project prices or personal data into public AI tools (for example free online chatbots).
- AI-generated content in a deliverable (text, code or calculations) must be reviewed by the engineer like any other input and is still subject to the Prepared / Checked / Approved review process.
- Engineering software and scripts written with AI help must be verified against a hand calculation or a benchmark before use on a project.

## 6. Software
Only software from the Contoso Software Centre may be installed. Requests for new engineering software are made through the IT Service Desk and need the line manager's approval. Licences for specialist software (for example finite element packages) are booked per project.
