# Project B: Costa Norte Offshore Wind - Substation Foundations Tender - Bid Strategy

Project / visibility tag: PROJECT B (CONFIDENTIAL, restricted to the bid team and admins)
Client: Costa Norte Renewables Consortium (CNRC)
Bid Manager: Rodrigo Faria | Technical Lead: Dr. Helena Duarte
Reference: CNW-CON-BID-0003 | Revision: P04 DRAFT | Last updated: 29 September 2026

## 1. Opportunity
CNRC is tendering the detailed design of the jacket foundations for two offshore substations at the Costa Norte offshore wind farm (water depth 45 to 55 m). The design contract runs for 18 months from contract award. The tender submission deadline is 6 November 2026 at 12:00. Clarification questions close on 16 October 2026.

## 2. Pricing (strictly confidential, do not share outside the bid team)
- Target price: 6.8 million EUR.
- Walk-away price: 5.9 million EUR. Below this price the contract is loss-making and the bid must not be submitted without Board approval.
- Planned margin at the target price: 12 percent.
- We expect the main competitor, Meridian Marine Engineering, to bid between 6.2 and 7.1 million EUR.

## 3. Win themes
- Proven experience: Contoso designed jacket foundations for the Ilha Azul offshore substation, delivered on time in 2025.
- Lower risk for the client: a fixed price for the design, with a cap on change-order rates.
- Local presence: design team in Lisbon, able to attend client workshops in person within 24 hours.
- Offer a 24-month design liability extension beyond the contract minimum.

## 4. Proposed key staff
- Project Director: Rodrigo Faria.
- Technical Lead: Dr. Helena Duarte (offshore structures, 15 years of experience).
- Geotechnical specialist: to be confirmed; a partner firm is being considered because the in-house geotechnical team is fully booked.

## 5. Risks
- The client's seabed survey data is incomplete; we may need to assume a conservative soil profile, which increases steel weight and could make our design less competitive.
- Key staff availability from Q2 2027 must be confirmed with Resourcing by 20 October 2026.

## 6. Bid programme
1. Bid/no-bid confirmation by the Board: 9 October 2026.
2. Cost model frozen: 23 October 2026.
3. Red-team review of the technical proposal: 28 October 2026.
4. Final price approval by the Managing Director: 4 November 2026.
5. Submission through the CNRC tender portal: 6 November 2026, 12:00.
