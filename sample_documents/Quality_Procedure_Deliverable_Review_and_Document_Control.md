# Contoso Engineering Consultants: Deliverable Review and Document Control Procedure

Project / visibility tag: GENERAL (all employees)
Document owner: Quality Manager, Joana Pires (quality@contoso.example)
Reference: QMS-PRO-012 | Revision: C05 | Part of the ISO 9001 quality management system

## 1. Purpose
This procedure makes sure that every report, drawing, calculation and model sent to a client has been checked and approved. It applies to all projects.

## 2. The three signatures (Prepared, Checked, Approved)
Every deliverable issued outside Contoso needs three different people:
- Prepared by: the engineer or designer who produced it.
- Checked by: an engineer who did not work on it, with at least the same grade as the author. The checker reviews the inputs, method, results and presentation.
- Approved by: the Design Lead or the Project Director, who confirms the deliverable meets the client brief.
The same person may never prepare and check the same deliverable. For calculations involving structural safety, the checker must be a chartered engineer.

## 3. Review timescales
- Plan at least 3 working days for checking a report and 5 working days for a calculation package.
- Checker comments are recorded on the Review Comment Sheet (form QMS-F-07). Each comment must be closed by the author before approval.

## 4. Revision codes
- P01, P02, ...: preliminary issues, for information or comment.
- C01, C02, ...: issues for construction or contractual use. A C revision requires all three signatures.
- A deliverable revised after a C issue gets the next C number; never reuse a revision code.

## 5. Document numbering
Documents are numbered PROJECT-ORIGINATOR-TYPE-NUMBER, for example LVM-CON-RPT-0042.
- Type codes: RPT (report), DWG (drawing), CAL (calculation), SPC (specification), MIN (minutes).
- Numbers are taken from the project document register, never invented.

## 6. Issuing to the client
- Deliverables are issued only through the project Common Data Environment (CDE), never by email attachment.
- Every issue is recorded on a transmittal, signed by the Document Controller.

## 7. Non-conformities
If a deliverable is found to have been issued without the required checks, report it to quality@contoso.example within 2 working days. The Quality Manager opens a non-conformity report and agrees the corrective action with the Project Director.
