Reference: LVM-CON-MIN-0019

## 1. Alameda Norte station
- The client asked to move the north entrance 12 m east to avoid an existing water main. Ines confirmed the new position works structurally, but the change affects the station box excavation sequence.
- Decision: the entrance relocation is accepted in principle, subject to an approved Change Request (form LVM-F-03). Marta will raise it with VVM.

## 2. Bored tunnel lining
- Rui presented the segment design for the bored tunnel: precast concrete segments, 350 mm thick, 1.5 m wide, 6+1 segment ring.
- The check of the lining design under fire is still open. The checker is waiting for the fire load assumptions from the client's fire engineer.
- Decision: the tunnel lining calculation package will be issued at P02 for comment only. It will not go to C01 until the fire check is closed.

## 3. Ventilation shaft VS-2
- Sofia reported that the new borehole BH-27 found softer clay than expected between 18 m and 24 m depth. The settlement prediction for the two buildings next to VS-2 increases from 8 mm to about 14 mm, which is within the amber trigger level.
- Decision: the shaft design will add jet-grout ground improvement around the excavation. Daily monitoring of those two buildings will start before shaft excavation begins.

## 4. Programme
- The Alameda Norte C01 issue date of 30 November 2026 is at risk because of the entrance relocation. A revised date will be agreed once the change request is decided.
