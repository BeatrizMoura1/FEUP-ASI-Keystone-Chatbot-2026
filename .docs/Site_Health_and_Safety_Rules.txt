CONTOSO ENGINEERING CONSULTANTS - SITE HEALTH AND SAFETY RULES

Project / visibility tag: GENERAL (all employees)
Document owner: Head of Health & Safety, Carlos Mota (safety@contoso.example)
Reference: HS-POL-001 | Revision: C07

1. BEFORE A SITE VISIT
- Every employee must complete the Contoso online site safety induction before their first site visit. It is valid for 2 years.
- Book every site visit in the SafeSite app at least 48 hours in advance, with the site name, the purpose of the visit and the expected return time.
- Read the contractor's site rules and attend the contractor's site induction on arrival. Their rules apply in addition to ours.

2. PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
Minimum PPE on any construction site:
- safety helmet with chin strap
- high-visibility vest or jacket (class 2 or higher)
- safety boots with toe cap and midsole protection
- safety glasses
- gloves
Additional PPE for tunnels and shafts: ear defenders, a self-rescuer and a personal gas detector, provided by the site.
PPE is ordered through the office manager and paid for by the company. Do not visit a site without it.

3. LONE WORKING
- Nobody visits a site alone without a check-in arrangement. Check in on the SafeSite app on arrival and check out when you leave.
- If you have not checked out 1 hour after your expected return time, the duty safety coordinator will call you, and then your emergency contact.

4. STOP WORK AUTHORITY
Every Contoso employee has the authority and the duty to stop any work they believe is unsafe. Nobody will be criticised for stopping work in good faith.

5. INCIDENTS AND NEAR MISSES
- Report every accident, injury or near miss in the SafeSite app within 24 hours.
- Serious incidents (hospital treatment, collapse, fire) must be reported by phone immediately to the 24-hour safety line: +351 210 000 199.

6. FITNESS FOR WORK
Alcohol and drugs are strictly forbidden on site. Employees taking medication that may affect alertness must tell their line manager before a site visit.
