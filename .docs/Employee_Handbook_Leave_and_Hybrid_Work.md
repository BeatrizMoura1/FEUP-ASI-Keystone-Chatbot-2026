# Contoso Engineering Consultants: Employee Handbook, Leave and Hybrid Work

Project / visibility tag: GENERAL (all employees)
Document owner: People & Culture (people@contoso.example)
Reference: HR-POL-004 | Revision: C03 | Effective from: 1 January 2026

## 1. Working hours
- The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday.
- Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00. Outside core hours, employees may organise their time flexibly, as long as client meetings and site commitments are respected.
- All hours must be recorded in the timesheet system by 12:00 every Monday for the previous week. Each hour must be booked to a project code or to an overhead code (OH-100 for training, OH-200 for internal meetings, OH-300 for bid work).

## 2. Annual leave
- Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus their birthday as an extra day off.
- After 5 years of service, annual leave increases to 27 days.
- Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, otherwise they are lost.
- Leave requests must be submitted in PeopleHub at least 10 working days in advance for absences of 3 days or more, and are approved by the line manager.
- Leave of more than 10 consecutive working days during a project's critical phase also needs the Project Director's approval.
- The office is closed between 24 December and 1 January. These days do not count against annual leave.

## 3. Sick leave
- Inform your line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence, by phone or Teams message.
- A medical certificate is required from the 4th consecutive day of sickness.
- Record the absence in PeopleHub on the day you return to work.

## 4. Parental and family leave
- Contoso follows national law for parental leave and tops up salary to 100 percent during the first 120 days of parental leave.
- Employees may take up to 3 paid days per year of family emergency leave (for example, a sick child or an urgent care situation).

## 5. Hybrid work
- Employees work from the office at least 3 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are team days, when everyone in the team is expected in the office.
- Site days count as office days.
- Remote work from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per year, only with prior approval from People & Culture and the line manager, submitted at least 15 working days before travel. Laptops must always connect through the company VPN.
- The company pays a home-office allowance of 30 EUR per month, included in the salary.

## 6. Learning and development
- Every employee has a training budget of 1,200 EUR per year and 5 training days, booked with code OH-100.
- Chartered engineer applications (for example to the Ordem dos Engenheiros) are fully paid by the company, including membership fees.
